# The Forgotten Lands — layout

**The map is inside the castle.** Not a road toward it: the walls are already
around you when you spawn, and everything that happens, happens in here.

## Shape

A **376 × 2250** enclosure (300 × 1800 until 2026-10-19, then widened 25% both
ways), sealed on all four sides, divided into **nine chambers** by cross-walls.
The numbers live in `configs/Castle.luau`; the tool that builds the walls and the
service that gates them both read them from there. Each cross-wall has ONE gap, and the gap alternates
side to side, so the route runs forward, cross right, forward, cross left,
forward — a serpentine you cannot get lost in and cannot leave.

```
   gate ──►  [1]═╗
                 ║      ╔═[3]  ──►
             ╔═[2]      ║
             ║          ╚═[4]
             ...              ...   [9] the end
```

There are **no invisible walls anywhere**. The castle is the boundary, which is
the only kind of boundary a player ever accepts.

Each chamber is **250 deep** with about **340 × 210** of open floor inside the
walls — an arena, sized for a wave. Passages sit 35 in from the side walls
(x ±153). The walls, gates and towers are generated by
`tools/BuildBattleground.luau` (MODE "resize" changes the size of a built castle
without losing anything in it); all nine chambers are furnished by
`tools/BuildWards.luau` (the older `tools/BuildChambers.luau` layout is gone).

**Every raised floor can be reached by the dead.** Lofts, upper storeys, the
guardhouse deck, the watch platform and the granary are all reached by stairs
whose walking surface is an invisible ramp, every doorway has steps with a ramp
under them, and `EnemyAIService` follows the floor and routes up them. Nothing
climbable reaches the height of the curtain walls.

## Markers

Every chamber carries the markers the gameplay systems already read, so a wave
can be pointed at a room without anyone typing a coordinate:

| Marker | Used by |
|---|---|
| `Spawns.North` / `East` / `West` | Encounter spawn points |
| `Objectives.Centre` | Reach / Defend / Interact objectives |
| `Checkpoint` | Where a rejoining player returns to |
| `Storyteller` | Where a story NPC stands |

Referenced from config by name, e.g. `Battleground.Chamber3.Spawns.North`.

## Pacing

Story first, then fighting.

1. **Arrival** — black screen, the title, then the player is standing inside the
   gate with the castle already around them.
2. **Chambers 1–2** — storyteller NPCs. What happened here, told by people who
   were here. No enemies yet.
3. **Chambers 3–8** — waves. Another storyteller every third or fourth wave, so
   the story keeps arriving instead of being front-loaded.
4. **Chamber 9** — the last of the story, and where it ends.

## Outside the walls

Scenery only: nothing out here can be reached in play, so nothing collides. It has two audiences — the
arrival scene, shot from the hill, and a player inside looking at the skyline over the battlements.

- **Moat**: one ring, 80 wide, inner edge 40 off the curtain wall (x ±228, z 60 / −2246), outer edge at
  x ±308, z 140 / −2326 (derived from `configs/Castle.luau`). Water at y −6. Built by `tools/BuildLand.luau` (terrain) and
  `tools/BuildSurroundings.luau` (stone face, reeds, debris, mist).
- **Bridge**: x ±15 from the gate (z 66) to the far bank (z 148). Drawbridge z 66–92, stone beyond.
- **The hill**: centred on (−40, 820), top at y ≈ 44, 800 studs in front of the gate, with the sight
  line to the gate kept clear. The party stands at `Surroundings.Intro.Stand1..6`.
- **Land**: flat for 100 studs past the moat, hills beyond, mountains 1.3k–1.9k out on every side.
- **What it says**: rails ending at a buffer stop, a lit shrine, a burnt village (left of the road), an
  old battlefield with its graves (right), a gallows, dead woods — and smoke inside the walls, and one
  red light in the last tower.

## Arrival

`IntroService` runs the scene once per server: the party on the hill, six lines said by the players
(by display name), then black, then the arrival pad. See `configs/Intro.luau`.

## Towers

The towers are generated with full medieval anatomy — batter, machicolations,
bartizans, stair turrets, cross loops, garderobes. See
[TowerAnatomy.md](TowerAnatomy.md) for what each piece is and why it is there.
No two are alike: heights, ruin, roofs and turret positions vary per chamber, so
the two flanks never match and the eye has something to follow.
